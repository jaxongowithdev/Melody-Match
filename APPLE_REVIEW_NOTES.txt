REVIEW NOTES — Tideframe

Tideframe is a complete offline teacher. Complete four onboarding steps and tap Start my Walk. On Walk tap Walk this session, then Start session. Pause or use Next step; Finish practice and choose a feeling to save locally. Open the Four columns quick tile and tap Start breathing to view rising columns, phase countdown and four rounds; Pause breathing stops the timer. Use the top destination strip to open Range, search or filter practices, or switch to Short lessons and open one. Rest offers Evening inventory and Quiet horizon. Open Tablet from the top strip or Write in Tablet on Walk, tap New note, choose a prompt and feeling, enter text and tap Save note. The dated card appears in Tablet and persists after reopening. Filter saved notes by feeling. No account, login, cloud sync or remote AI; no credentials required. All teaching and journal functions work offline.
