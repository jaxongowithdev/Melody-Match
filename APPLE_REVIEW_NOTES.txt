REVIEW NOTES — Ember Atlas

Ember Atlas is a complete offline teacher. Complete four onboarding screens and tap Start my Walk. On Walk tap Walk this session, then Start session. Pause or use Next step; Finish practice and choose a feeling to save locally. In Walk station 02 open Four columns and tap Start breathing to observe four rising columns, phase countdown and four rounds. Pause breathing stops the timer. Open the navigation menu in the top-left to choose Range, Rest or Tablet; on wider screens these destinations appear in a sidebar. In Range expand a category, search a route, or switch to Short lessons, expand one and tap Open lesson. Rest includes Three gentle endings and Resting outline. In Tablet tap New note, choose a prompt and feeling, enter text and tap Save note. Search saved notes and expand Reflection prompt. Notes persist locally after reopening. No credentials, account, login, cloud sync or remote AI. All teaching and journal functions work offline.
