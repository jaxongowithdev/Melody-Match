REVIEW NOTES — Soft Margin

Soft Margin is a complete offline teacher. Complete four onboarding steps and tap Start my Walk. On Walk, tap Walk this session, then Start session. Pause or use Next step; Finish practice and choose a mood to save locally. Open Four columns from Walk and tap Start breathing to observe rising columns, four phases and four rounds; Pause breathing stops the timer. Use the bottom navigation to open Range, search or filter practices, switch to Short lessons and open a lesson. Rest offers Three enoughs and Resting layers. Open Tablet from the bottom navigation or Write in Tablet on Walk. Enter a note and tap Save note. It appears under Your Pages with its prompt and mood and persists locally after reopening. No account, login, cloud sync or remote AI. No credentials required; all teaching and journal functions work offline.
