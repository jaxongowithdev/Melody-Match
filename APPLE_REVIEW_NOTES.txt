REVIEW NOTES — Mosslight

Mosslight is a complete offline teacher. Complete four onboarding steps and tap Start my Walk. On Walk, tap Walk this session, then Start session. Pause or use Next step; on the final step tap Finish practice and choose a mood to save the practice locally. On Walk, open Four columns and tap Start breathing. Observe four rising columns, phase countdowns and four rounds; Pause breathing stops the timer. Open Range, filter practices and open a quick field lesson. Rest contains two wind-down sessions. Open Tablet, choose a prompt and mood, enter an observation and tap Save note. The saved note appears under Collected observations and persists locally. Test in Airplane Mode: there is no account, login, cloud sync or remote AI. No credentials required.
