REVIEW NOTES — Cadence Room

Cadence Room is a complete offline teacher. Complete the four onboarding screens and tap Start my Walk. Use the top tabs to navigate. On Walk, tap Walk this session, then Start session. Pause or use Next step, then Finish practice and choose a mood to save the practice locally. On Walk, open Four columns and tap Start breathing to see rising columns, phase countdowns and four rounds. Pause breathing stops the timer. Open Range to filter sessions and open a skill card. Rest offers Closing shift and Quiet playback. Open Tablet, choose a prompt and mood, enter a note and tap Save note. The note appears under Saved recaps and persists locally after reopening. No account, login, cloud sync or remote AI. No credentials required. All teaching and journal functions work offline.
