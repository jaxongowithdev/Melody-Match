Find your own rhythm. Ten guided practices, rising breath columns and a private collection of moments. Fully offline. No account. No cloud.

DESCRIPTION
Tideframe is a complete offline teacher for comfortable movement, attention, breathing and rest. Ten original guided practices, five short lessons and three breathing rhythms are included on your device. No account, no login, no cloud sync and no remote AI.

YOUR PRACTICE DASHBOARD
Choose Reset, Mobilize, Focus or Unwind during onboarding, then set an 8, 12 or 18 minute window and a comfortable pace. Walk puts your recommended practice beside quick access to Four columns and Tablet, with locally stored practice-day and weekly-minute totals.

FOLLOW A GUIDED WALK
Explore Sound compass, Near and far, Heel and toe, Hand fan, Side space, Grounded reach, Breath landmarks and One thing at a time. Each session has four written, timed steps, a circular countdown, pause and next-step controls. Save a feeling when you finish. Rest offers Evening inventory and Quiet horizon in a dimmed player.

FOUR COLUMNS
Four rising columns guide inhale, pause, exhale and pause. Phase labels, countdown and round progress keep the rhythm clear. Easy current and Level tide are alternatives without breath holds. Keep the breath comfortable; pause or stop whenever you need.

EXPLORE RANGE
Search and filter the practice collection, or switch to Short lessons. Learn a setup, an ordinary breathing cue and a comfortable alternative. The collection adapts to one or two columns according to available space.

CAPTURE A MOMENT IN TABLET
Tap New note to open a separate writing panel. Choose a prompt and feeling, write your observation and save it locally. Browse dated note cards, revisit the original prompt or filter by feeling. Notes remain available after reopening. Private journal — fully offline. No account. No cloud.

Choose Day current, Night current or your device appearance. All teaching, preferences, practice history and journal functions work without a network connection.

REVIEW NOTES
Tideframe is a complete offline teacher. Complete four onboarding steps and tap Start my Walk. On Walk tap Walk this session, then Start session. Pause or use Next step; Finish practice and choose a feeling to save locally. Use the top destination strip to open Range, search or filter practices, or switch to Short lessons and open one. Rest offers Evening inventory and Quiet horizon. Open Tablet from the top strip or Write in Tablet on Walk, tap New note, choose a prompt and feeling, enter text and tap Save note. The dated card appears in Tablet and persists after reopening. Filter saved notes by feeling. No account, login, cloud sync or remote AI; no credentials required. All teaching and journal functions work offline.
