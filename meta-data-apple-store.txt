PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Find your rhythm with musical matching puzzles. Explore 24 levels, discover eight instruments, enjoy a daily encore and collect rewards. Play entirely offline.

DESCRIPTION
A little rhythm. A little magic. Melody Match is a colorful instrument-matching game with a growing musical journey. Tap two identical instruments, collect every pair and celebrate your next performance.

YOUR GRAND JOURNEY
Explore 24 levels across The Practice Room, Moonlight Theater and Orchestra Hall. The countdown becomes shorter as you progress. Earn up to three stars, improve your personal best and unlock the next performance. Time remaining adds a score bonus.

A DAILY ENCORE
Play an arrangement based on the local date with a 90-second countdown. Complete it to earn a 150-coin bonus once per day. Replay the daily board whenever you like.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN TEMPO
Free play removes the countdown so you can enjoy finding pairs without a time limit. Pause a performance, restart a board or return home at any time.

MEET YOUR ORCHESTRA
Discover eight instruments by matching them during play. Visit the Collection to see your pair totals and read a short introduction to each instrument, from violin and flute to saxophone and banjo.

REWARDS AND BOOSTERS
Earn coins for matching pairs, clearing a journey level for the first time and completing milestones. Spend earned coins in the backstage shop on hints, shuffles, ten-second time freezes and automatic matches. No real-money purchases.

YOUR PROGRESS, ON YOUR DEVICE
Levels, best scores, stars, coins, boosters, collection progress, claimed rewards and sound preferences are saved locally. View your statistics and instructions in Settings. No account, ads or cloud backup. All game features work offline. An unfinished board starts again when reopened; collected rewards and progression remain saved.

REVIEW NOTES
Melody Match opens on Home. Tap PLAY LEVEL 1 to begin, then tap two identical instrument tiles. Match all eight pairs to finish and unlock level 2. Journey has 24 progressively shorter timed levels and records stars and best scores. Use the back arrow to return Home. The bottom navigation opens Home, Journey, Collection, Quests and Shop. Locked levels cannot be opened until the preceding level is completed.

Home > Daily encore opens a detail page and a date-seeded 90-second board. The first daily win grants 150 coins; repeats do not duplicate the bonus. Home > Free play opens an untimed board. Freeze is disabled in Free play. The game includes Hint, Shuffle, Freeze, Auto match, pause/resume, sound toggle and restart confirmation.

Collection shows eight instruments; tap a card to open its educational detail page. Match an instrument to mark it discovered. Quests awards milestone coins through a Claim reward button, once per milestone. Shop exchanges earned in-game coins for boosters, with unaffordable purchases disabled. There is no in-app purchasing, login or advertising.

The settings gear opens sound preferences, local statistics, instructions, save information and a confirmed reset action. Progress persists locally in a separate Melody Match SQLite database. No account, credentials or network connection are required. Previously bundled Ember Atlas screens are not exposed by the new app entry point.
