A small path back to yourself. Follow a guided route, breathe with four rising columns and leave a private trace. Fully offline. No account. No cloud.

DESCRIPTION
Ember Atlas is a complete offline teacher for comfortable movement, attention, breathing and rest. Ten original guided routes, five field skills and three breathing patterns are included on your device. No account, no login, no cloud sync and no remote AI.

FIND YOUR STARTING POINT
Complete onboarding by choosing Arrive, Move, Attend or Release, an 8, 12 or 18 minute window and a comfortable pace. Walk presents a three-station route: arrive and practice, follow the breath, then leave a trace in Tablet. Recommendations and practice totals are computed locally.

TEN GUIDED ROUTES
Explore Texture trail, Room landmarks, Wrist orbit, Seated march, Elbow windows, Knee glide, Breath weather and Return markers. Each route contains four written, timed steps, a visible route checklist, countdown, pause and next-step controls. Save a feeling when you finish. Rest offers Three gentle endings and Resting outline in a dimmed player.

FOUR COLUMNS
Four arch-shaped columns rise and fall through inhale, pause, exhale and pause. Read the method, start or pause, and follow the phase countdown and round number. Gentle glow and Steady lantern offer patterns without holds. Keep breathing comfortable and return to your own rhythm whenever needed.

RANGE: THE ROUTE INDEX
Open a category folder or search by name and description. Switch to Short lessons to expand a field skill, read the setup and breathing cue, then open its comfortable alternative. Choose your own route whenever you prefer.

TABLET: TRACES OF PRACTICE
Tap New note to open a full-screen writing space. Choose a reflection prompt and feeling, write a note and save it on your device. Browse dated traces, expand the original prompt and search your notes by words, prompt or feeling. Saved notes remain available after reopening. Private journal — fully offline. No account. No cloud.

The side menu becomes a persistent sidebar on wider screens. Choose Ember night, Limestone or your device appearance. All teaching, preferences, practice history and journal functions work without a network connection.

REVIEW NOTES
Ember Atlas is a complete offline teacher. Complete four onboarding screens and tap Start my Walk. On Walk tap Walk this session, then Start session. Pause or use Next step; Finish practice and choose a feeling to save locally. In Walk station 02 open Four columns and tap Start breathing to observe four rising columns, phase countdown and four rounds. Pause breathing stops the timer. Open the navigation menu in the top-left to choose Range, Rest or Tablet; on wider screens these destinations appear in a sidebar. In Range expand a category, search a route, or switch to Short lessons, expand one and tap Open lesson. Rest includes Three gentle endings and Resting outline. In Tablet tap New note, choose a prompt and feeling, enter text and tap Save note. Search saved notes and expand Reflection prompt. Notes persist locally after reopening. No credentials, account, login, cloud sync or remote AI. All teaching and journal functions work offline.
