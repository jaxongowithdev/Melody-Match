DESCRIPTION
Cadence Room is a complete offline teacher for the pauses between everyday tasks. Learn small skills for resetting attention, moving comfortably and winding down. Ten original guided sessions, five skill cards and three breathing rhythms are included on your device. No account, no login, no cloud sync and no remote AI.

BUILD YOUR DAILY MIX
Complete a short onboarding: choose Reset, Loosen, Attend or Unwind, set 8, 12 or 18 minutes, and choose your energy level. Walk recommends a session using these preferences, the time of day and recent practice. Start a timer, pause when needed, move to the next step and save how you feel at the end.

PRACTICE A TRANSITION
The arrival ritual, Screen intermission, Elbow room, Seated steps, Side space, Single channel, Gentle interval and Between two tasks offer written guidance for everyday breaks. Range lets you browse by category and study five short skill cards with setup, breathing and easier alternatives. Rest contains Closing shift and Quiet playback for an unhurried evening.

BREATH STUDIO
Four columns guides four rounds of inhale, pause, exhale and pause with four rising meters. Steady tempo and Longer out offer rhythms without holds. See the current phase, countdown and round, and pause the exercise whenever you like.

TABLET: YOUR PRIVATE RECAP
Choose a prompt and mood, write a note and save it locally. Read dated recaps after reopening the app. Notes, preferences and practice history stay on this device. Private journal — fully offline. No account. No cloud.

A porcelain-and-cobalt interface, warm orange rhythm markers and Day studio or Night studio appearance make your next pause easy to find.
