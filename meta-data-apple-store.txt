DESCRIPTION
Soft Margin is a complete offline teacher for comfortable movement, attention, breathing and rest. A warm-paper notebook with ten original guided practices, five short lessons and three breathing patterns. All teaching is included on your device. No account, no login, no cloud sync and no remote AI.

YOUR DAILY PAGE
Complete onboarding by choosing Settle, Open, Notice or Rest, a practice window of 8, 12 or 18 minutes, and a comfortable pace. Walk offers a three-part path: make space with a guided session, follow an easy breathing rhythm, then leave a note in Tablet. Recommendations use your preferences, time of day and recent practice.

TEN GUIDED PRACTICES
Explore The weight of palms, Room around the jaw, Small shoulder arcs, The chair hinge, A quiet turn, Edge and centre, Patient breath and The unwritten page. Each practice has written teaching, four timed steps, pause and next-step controls. Rest offers Three enoughs and Resting layers for an unhurried evening.

RANGE: THE CONTENTS
Search practices and short lessons by name or description. Filter practices by category, or switch to Short lessons to study setup, breathing and a comfortable alternative. Choose your own lesson whenever you prefer.

FOUR COLUMNS
Four rising columns guide inhale, pause, exhale and pause. Open margin and Even lines offer patterns without holds. Read the method, start or pause, and follow the phase countdown and round number. Keep the breath comfortable and return to ordinary breathing whenever you need.

TABLET: PERSONAL PAGES
Choose a reflection prompt and mood, write a note and tap Save note. Your dated pages show the original prompt and your words. Notes, preferences and practice history are stored locally and remain available after reopening the app. Private journal — fully offline. No account. No cloud.

Choose Warm paper or Evening ink appearance, or match your device. Your next page is always available without a network connection.
